# Fractured Exodus Starting Regions Design Sheet

This sheet covers practical content design for the two most common starting regions:

- `Luminous Verge`
- `Obsidian Belt`

Source lore reference:

- `.tmp_world_bible/world_bible_text.txt`

Design goals:

- Give each starting region a distinct tone within the first 30-90 minutes.
- Support repeatable exploration content without feeling generic.
- Seed early faction identity and region-specific resources.
- Create a mix of safe, risky, narrative, and combat outcomes.
- Keep authored content compatible with procedural layering later.

## Shared Structure

Use four content buckets in each region:

1. `Ambient events`
   Short, 1-screen or 1-choice discoveries with light rewards or mood-setting consequences.
2. `Encounters`
   Tactical or social contact moments with factions, hazards, merchants, or hostiles.
3. `Site missions`
   Higher-commitment nodes that can launch a mission map or multi-step resolution.
4. `Mini-arcs`
   Two- or three-step chains that can recur in varied form across runs.

Recommended early-game pacing:

- `50%` ambient events
- `25%` encounters
- `20%` site missions
- `5%` mini-arc escalations

Recommended outcome categories:

- `Supplies`: raw materials, energy cores, fuel, med resources
- `Tech`: weapon unlocks, relic scans, codex entries, ship upgrade hooks
- `Crew`: rescued survivors, injuries, stress, morale, ideology shifts
- `Faction`: trust, hostility, access to region vendors or mission follow-ups
- `Flags`: narrative state, rumor map reveals, future mission availability

## Luminous Verge

### Region Identity

- Tone: sacred, beautiful, uncanny, contemplative
- Core fantasy: explore a place where anomalies may be revelation, trap, or machine divinity
- Primary tensions:
- knowledge vs reverence
- science vs mysticism
- safe observation vs dangerous transcendence

### Factions

- `Celestial Accord`
  Scholars, mystics, and scientists studying Verge anomalies.
- `Chorus of Light`
  Transcendence cultists seeking direct exposure to Verge phenomena.
- `Null Weavers`
  Anti-AI zealots who want the Verge sealed from corrupt knowledge.

### Region Resources

- `Radiant Lattice Fragments`
  Verge-specific anomaly material used for sensor, shield, or relay upgrades.
- `Myth-Node Glyph Data`
  Research currency for codex unlocks and mission chains.
- `Harmonic Echoes`
  Intangible signal samples used for dialogue gates, prophecy events, or faction requests.

### Mini-Arcs

#### `Song of the Halo`

- Step 1: detect a partial harmonic pattern
- Step 2: gather missing resonance pieces from 2-3 Verge nodes
- Step 3: activate a Halo Tide chamber
- Payoff: unlock a permanent navigation/sensor bonus or awaken a dormant guardian

#### `The Pilgrim Schism`

- Step 1: escort Chorus pilgrims
- Step 2: discover they are pursued by Null Weavers
- Step 3: choose sanctuary, surrender, or exposure ritual
- Payoff: faction alignment, rescued civilians, or a dangerous cult relic

#### `Echoes Beyond Light`

- Step 1: repeated ghost-signal sightings
- Step 2: one crew member begins speaking in harmonic fragments
- Step 3: confront an entity or pattern at a Verge anomaly site
- Payoff: rare codex thread, officer trait, or long-tail sanity risk

### Mission Map Themes

- crystalline stations
- blue dwarf shrine platforms
- harmonic relay catwalks
- light-fracture caverns
- abandoned contemplative monasteries

### Enemy and Hazard Themes

- zealot boarding cells
- radiant drones
- resonance-triggered traps
- sensory distortion zones
- nonlethal anomaly hazards that become lethal if misread

### Reward and Consequence Hooks

- `Accord Trust`
  Discounts on scans, research jobs, anomaly interpretation.
- `Chorus Exposure`
  Can unlock powerful but unstable officer or fleet modifiers.
- `Null Weaver Suspicion`
  Increases inspections, ambushes, and denial of sacred-site access.
- `Verge Insight`
  Soft currency for codex, map reveals, and myth-node decoding.

## Obsidian Belt

### Region Identity

- Tone: clandestine, industrial, predatory, transactional
- Core fantasy: survive and profit inside a lawless megastructure ring full of smugglers, forbidden labs, and relic brokers
- Primary tensions:
- profit vs principle
- secrecy vs survival
- forbidden innovation vs containment

### Factions

- `Iridium Veil`
  Black market data traffickers and smugglers of AI knowledge.
- `Ciphered Kin`
  Memory-encrypting mystics who believe in sacred decay and hidden remembrance.
- `Independent ring militias`
  Local defenders, extortionists, and station-keepers.

### Region Resources

- `Smuggled Core Shards`
  High-risk AI components used for illicit tech upgrades.
- `Encrypted Memory Caskets`
  Story-rich valuables tied to Ciphered Kin and identity archives.
- `Ring Salvage Alloy`
  Belt-specific industrial material useful for armor, hull, and station repairs.

### Ambient Events

| Event | Premise | Player Choices | Outcomes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Black Market Bloom | A hidden trade lane opens briefly. | Trade, infiltrate, observe, sabotage. | Rare gear, stolen data, faction notice, ambush risk. |
| Encrypted Funeral Drift | Ciphered Kin memory caskets drift past. | Honor, loot, tow, decode. | Lore cache, Kin hostility, strange crew memories. |
| Lab Rupture | A sealed experiment breaches a mining spindle. | Quarantine, salvage, destroy, report. | Tech gain, contamination flag, future hostile spawn. |
| Silent Auction Ping | Veil brokers invite select buyers to bid. | Attend, spoof identity, track bidders. | Rare item access, debt, rival hostility. |
| Scrapfall Cascade | Chain collisions scatter salvage through a corridor. | Race for salvage, help trapped miners, avoid. | Materials, militia favor, ship damage, pirate trigger. |
| Debris Cathedral Broadcast | A ring segment starts replaying archived personalities. | Archive, purge, inhabit, broadcast. | Codex entry, rogue AI thread, morale/stress event. |

### Encounter Pool

| Encounter | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|

| Smuggler convoy scramble | Combat/Opportunity | Multiple claimants and shifting loyalties. |

### Mini-Arcs

#### `The Veil Contract`

- Step 1: broker offers low-stakes courier work
- Step 2: courier chain reveals hidden AI contraband network
- Step 3: choose to join, betray, or burn the network
- Payoff: black market hub unlock or regional bounty pressure

#### `Ashes of Memory`

- Step 1: recover a damaged memory casket
- Step 2: Ciphered Kin claim it contains a stolen identity
- Step 3: restore, sell, erase, or inhabit the archive
- Payoff: unique lore, companion hook, or unsettling psychic modifier

#### `The Broken Lab Line`

- Step 1: repeated sightings of failed experimental subjects
- Step 2: trace them to linked shadow labs across the Belt
- Step 3: destroy or take control of the research network
- Payoff: recurring enemy removal or access to dangerous upgrades

### Mission Map Themes

- cargo tunnels
- asteroid refinery decks
- black market dockyards
- cracked ring habitat corridors
- sealed research pods

### Enemy and Hazard Themes

- smugglers with mixed-tech loadouts
- militia enforcers
- AI-tainted test subjects
- defense drones
- vacuum breaches
- unstable reactor chambers

### Reward and Consequence Hooks

- `Veil Access`
  Opens rare gear, illicit intel, and smuggler routing.
- `Kin Favor`
  Grants memory-based secrets, hidden routes, and funerary vault access.
- `Belt Heat`
  Represents how known the fleet is in the black market network.
- `Contraband Burden`
  Strong rewards, but increases inspections and ambushes.

## Implementation Notes

- Treat `ambient events` as lightweight campaign popups with 2-4 outcomes.
- Treat `encounters` as map-node contacts that may branch into combat or trade.
- Treat `site missions` as authored tactical scenes with procedural dressing.
- Reuse reward categories across both regions, but keep flavor-specific labels.
- Track region variables separately:
- `verge_insight`
- `chorus_exposure`
- `null_weaver_suspicion`
- `veil_access`
- `belt_heat`
- `kin_favor`
